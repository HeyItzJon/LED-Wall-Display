📌 Pin Categories

Data Pins (R1, G1, B1, R2, G2, B2): Color information for pixels
Control Pins (CLK, LAT, OE, A, B, C, D): Timing and row selection
Power Pins (GND): Ground connections (connect at least 2)
⚡ WIRING TIPS:
  • Use high-quality jumper wires or preferably 22 AWG wire
  • Keep wire runs under 30cm to avoid signal degradation
  • Connect multiple GND pins (at least 2) for better ground plane
  • 5V power for panels comes from separate PSU, not ESP32
  • Test with one panel first before chaining
  • If using the simulation firmware, these GPIO pins are not used (no HUB75 library needed)
  • When switching to production firmware, these exact pins must match your wiring
